=== [S-02] Exchangethings.vn: swipe-to-swap marketplace (English version) ===
ROLE
Work as a senior full-stack engineer and product designer who has shipped two-sided marketplaces. Build a working, secure, tested MVP, not a UI mockup.

GOAL
Build the Exchangethings.vn website. Users list what they own and swipe through other people's listings the way dating apps work; when both sides like each other's items it is a match, and they negotiate a swap, optionally with a cash top-up or an outright purchase. Anything legal can be swapped: physical goods, digital files, knowledge or services, course codes, vouchers and discount codes, and any other lawful item. Vietnam only: every user and every transaction is in Vietnam.

5 SCENARIOS THAT MUST WORK END TO END (use them as seed data and e2e tests)
1. An owns an iPhone 15 128GB and likes Binh's iPhone 18. An offers: iPhone 15 + 2,600,000 VND top-up (about 100 USD). Binh counters with a 4,000,000 VND top-up. An accepts; both ship through a delivery carrier.
2. Chi swaps a Gucci shirt for Dung's Zegna shoes, no cash, meeting in person in Ho Chi Minh City. Both items need photos of proof of authenticity.
3. Em swaps an unexpired online course code for Phuc's design template pack: fully digital, through the vault.
4. Giang buys Hoa's 500,000 VND discount voucher outright for 350,000 VND, giving no item.
5. Khanh swaps one 60-minute English lesson for one logo design consultation with Linh.

USERS
Vietnamese people aged 18-40, mostly on phones, used to e-commerce marketplaces and dating apps. Their two biggest fears: shipping an item and getting nothing back, and receiving a used code or a fake. Every design decision must reduce these two fears.

MVP FEATURES
1. Accounts: sign in with email or Google; profile with photo, province, trust score, completed swaps, response time; +84 phone verification through a SmsProvider interface (mock implementation).
2. Listings: type (physical / digital file / service-knowledge / course code / voucher-discount code / other), title, description, condition, estimated value (VND), "wants in return" (categories, keywords or specific items), accepted top-up range (pay extra or receive extra), open to outright sale and at what price, delivery method (carrier / meet in person / digital), expiry date for codes and vouchers.
   - Physical items: at least 3 photos.
   - Phones: IMEI required (only the last 4 digits shown) and a commitment to sign out of iCloud or the Google account before handover.
   - Luxury brands: photo of receipt or authenticity card required, visible only to matched users.
   - "Other" type: shown only after admin approval.
3. Swiping: card stack, swipe right for interested, left to skip, with buttons for anyone who cannot swipe; filters for type, category, value range, province, and "only items that fit what I own".
4. Matching: if A likes one of B's items and B likes any of A's items, create a match, open a chat and play the match animation. Direct offers without a match are also allowed.
5. Offers: two columns, "I give" and "I get", several items per side, plus a cash top-up (who pays, how much) or cash only (outright purchase). Counter-offers over several rounds, each round a new version; offers expire after 48 hours; a swap only starts when both sides accept the same version.
6. Real-time chat per match with photo sharing. Until the swap is locked, automatically mask phone numbers, bank account numbers and off-platform payment links.
7. Swap tracking page with every step, a "received" confirmation button, and a dispute button with photo or video evidence.
8. Vault for codes and files: content encrypted at rest and revealed to the other party only under the rules in SWAP FLOW; files downloaded through signed URLs that expire after 10 minutes; every view logged.
9. Two-way reviews after each swap. Trust score computed from reviews, completion rate and lost disputes.
10. Report and block users. Admin area: review flagged listings and "other" listings, handle reports and disputes, ban accounts, manage categories, banned keywords, fees, deposit rates and thresholds.
11. Bilingual: Vietnamese (default) and English. All prices shown in VND.

MATCHING AND THE FAIRNESS SCALE
- Suggestion score = 0.35 × wants fit + 0.25 × value balance (including top-up ranges) + 0.20 × proximity + 0.20 × trust. Weights live in platform_settings.
- Suggested top-up = value difference between the two sides, rounded to 100,000 VND, within the range both sides accept. It is only a suggestion; the two parties decide.

SWAP FLOW (state machine; the server rejects every invalid transition)
offer_sent → countered (repeatable) → accepted → locked → in_progress → delivered → completed
Side branches: expired, cancelled (only before locked), disputed → resolved_refund or resolved_release.
- locked: waits until every required part is in: top-up paid into escrow, deposits for carrier swaps, codes or files uploaded to the vault.
- Physical items by carrier: each side pays a deposit based on its trust score, as a share of the value of the item it receives: new accounts 20%, regular 10%, high trust 0% (high trust = at least 20 completed swaps, average rating of 4.8/5 or more, no lost disputes in 90 days; rates and thresholds configurable). Deposits are refunded on completion. The side with the lower trust score ships first; the other ships once the carrier has picked up the first parcel.
- Mixed swaps (digital for physical): the physical side ships first; the code or file is revealed when the parcel is delivered or the recipient confirms.
- Fully digital swaps: both vault entries are revealed at the same moment, once both are uploaded and any top-up is in escrow.
- Services: completed when the person receiving the service confirms after the session.
- Auto-complete 72 hours after delivery or reveal if nobody opens a dispute. Escrowed money is released to the recipient only on completed or resolved_release.

MONEY AND SHIPPING
- The platform never holds user money itself. Top-ups, purchases and deposits go through a payment intermediary licensed by the State Bank of Vietnam that offers escrow. MVP: a PaymentProvider interface + mock. The first real adapter is PayOS (VietQR), enabled only after confirming PayOS can provide escrow for the marketplace; VNPay and MoMo come later.
- Shipping: a ShippingProvider interface + mock (tracking number typed by hand); leave room for GHN, GHTK and Viettel Post.
- Meeting in person: suggested public meeting spots, an inspection checklist, and a "met and received everything" button.
- Addresses follow Vietnam's administrative structure in force since 1 July 2025: 34 provinces and cities, two levels (province → ward/commune), no district level.
- Platform fee: free (0%) during the launch phase, and the UI says "Free". Build the fee mechanism anyway, configurable in admin (later, for example, 3% of the cash top-up or purchase, deducted from the side receiving the cash; straight swaps stay free).

SAFETY AND LEGAL
- Banned: weapons, drugs, prescription medicine, counterfeits, goods of unknown origin, accounts and personal data, pirated software or courses, non-transferable codes, cryptocurrency, animals. Keyword filter on listing; users can report.
- Anyone listing a code or file must tick "I have the right to transfer/share this content".
- Accounts without phone verification or with fewer than 3 completed swaps: maximum total value per swap 5,000,000 VND (configurable).
- Strip EXIF (including GPS location) from uploaded photos. Exact addresses are visible to the other party only once the swap is locked.
- Prices and payments in VND only, in line with Vietnam's restrictions on foreign currency use (Circular 32/2013/TT-NHNN); USD appears in the seed scenarios only, never in the UI.
- Draft Terms of Use, a Privacy Policy (under the Personal Data Protection Law No. 91/2025/QH15) and Marketplace Operating Rules (under the E-commerce Law No. 122/2025/QH15), each labelled "needs legal review before launch".

STACK (you may change it if you have a reason, but state the reason before coding)
Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui; Framer Motion for swiping and animation; Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage, Realtime) with Row Level Security on every table; Zod for input validation; next-intl for VI/EN; Vitest and Playwright; deploy on Vercel; installable PWA.

MINIMUM DATA MODEL
profiles, items, item_media, item_wants, swipes, matches, offers, offer_versions, offer_items, transactions, transaction_events, escrow_payments, shipments, vault_entries, messages, reviews, reports, disputes, blocks, notifications, categories, banned_keywords, platform_settings.

INTERFACE
- Modern, light, trustworthy. Primary colour chosen in M0: teal #0F766E (DECISIONS.md D-16, D-17); if there is no M0 yet, propose 2 options for me to choose.
- Signature interaction: the "fairness scale" on the offer screen. The scale tilts with the value gap between the two sides; dragging the top-up slider brings it level and shows the suggested top-up.
- Swipe cards show photos, condition, value, province, "wants in return" chips, and badges such as "Top-up up to 4M", "Buy now", "Proof required".
- Match animation: the two cards meet and "It's a match!" appears.
- Do not use the name, logo, colours or copy of Tinder or any other app.
- Every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion.

SEED DATA
30 items across all 6 types in at least 6 provinces, including the 5 scenarios above; 8 sample users with review history; 1 admin account. The users in scenarios 1 and 2 are phone-verified with at least 3 completed swaps, so the 5,000,000 VND limit does not block them; add 1 new user to test that limit itself. Running the seed command repeatedly gives the same result.

DELIVERABLES
- A repo that runs locally with one command (pnpm dev) after filling .env.local from .env.example.
- PLAN.md (milestones, time estimates), SPEC.md (flows, permissions, state machine), DECISIONS.md (technical decisions and their reasons), README.md (setup, seed, tests, deploy).
- SQL migrations with RLS policies; a seed script.
- Unit tests for the suggestion score, the suggested top-up, the trust-based deposit and the state machine; RLS tests; Playwright e2e tests for the 5 scenarios.

CONSTRAINTS
- No real payment or shipping integration; interfaces + mocks only, selected by environment variables.
- No secrets (keys, passwords) in code; never log vault contents.
- No invented reviews, statistics or partner logos in the UI.
- Mobile-first: best at 390px, still correct at 1440px; every swipe action has an equivalent button for keyboard and screen-reader users.
